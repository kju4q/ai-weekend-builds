RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — FAKE DATA)

This lease is made between Northwind Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the
Tenant, for the property at 44 Maple Court, Apartment 3B.

TERM. The lease term begins on March 1, 2025 and ends on February 28, 2026.

RENT. Monthly rent is $1,850, due on the 1st of each month. A late fee of $75
applies if rent is not received by the 5th.

SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant paid a security deposit of $2,775, refundable
within 30 days of move-out, less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear.

BREAKING THE LEASE EARLY. If the Tenant terminates this lease before the end
of the term, the Tenant must give 60 days written notice and pay an early
termination fee equal to two months rent ($3,700). The deposit may not be used
to cover the early termination fee.

PETS. One cat or dog under 25 lbs is permitted with a one-time pet fee of $300.

RENEWAL. If neither party gives notice 45 days before the end of the term, the
lease automatically converts to a month-to-month tenancy at the same rent.
