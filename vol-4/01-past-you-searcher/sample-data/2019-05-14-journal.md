# Sunday, late

I have been circling this project for three weeks without starting it. The
worry is always the same shape: that I will spend two months on it and find out
nobody wanted it, and that the two months will have been the interesting part of
my year.

So I keep researching instead. Research feels like progress and costs nothing.
That is exactly why it is a trap.

What I actually believe, when I am honest: the only way to find out is to build
the smallest version that a real person can use, and put it in front of one
person who is not me. Everything else is procrastination wearing a nice outfit.

Note to future me: you were not blocked on information. You were blocked on the
fear of finding out.
