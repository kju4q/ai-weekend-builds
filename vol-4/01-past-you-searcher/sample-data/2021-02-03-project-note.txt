date: 2021-02-03

Project note, week 2.

Scope keeps growing. Every time I sit down I add a feature to the plan instead
of removing one. The plan is now four months long and I have written no code.

Rule I am adopting, starting today: the smallest version that a real person can
use, or nothing. If a feature does not survive that test, it goes in a list
called "later" that I am allowed to never read.

Second thing I keep relearning: ship it before it is ready, because "ready" is
a feeling and it arrives about three weeks after the thing was already good
enough. The version I am embarrassed by is the version that teaches me
something. The polished version that never shipped taught me nothing.

Building products is mostly the discipline of deleting your own ideas.
