## Why I am not sold on AI in my workflow

Everyone I follow has spent this year wiring language models into everything
they do, and I have spent it watching them and staying out.

My problem is not that the output is bad. My problem is that it is confidently
plausible. It hands you a paragraph shaped exactly like an answer, and checking
whether it is actually an answer costs about as much as writing it yourself
would have. That is not a tool, that is a second job.

I will change my mind the day one of these things can show me where an answer
came from. Until then I would rather be slow and know what I know.

Building products on top of a system that cannot cite itself feels like building
on sand, and I have done enough of that already.
