# What changed

Reread my post from 2022 about staying out of AI workflows. I still agree with
the reasoning and I no longer agree with the conclusion, which is a strange
thing to say about your own writing.

The reasoning was: a tool that cannot show its sources is not a tool. That was
right. What I got wrong was assuming that was a permanent property rather than a
missing feature.

What actually changed my mind was retrieval. Once the thing answers from
documents I chose, and points at the passage it used, checking it costs seconds
instead of costing as much as doing the work myself. The confidence stopped
being a problem the moment it became checkable.

So the position now: I do not want a model that knows things. I want a model
that can find the things I already wrote and quote them back to me accurately.
Narrower, and much more useful.

Still the same rule for building products, though. Smallest version a real
person can use. That one has not moved in five years.
