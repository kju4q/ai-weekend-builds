# 02. Pattern Mirror

**Difficulty**: Easy-Medium | **Time**: 3-5h

## What it is

Reads years of your camera roll and shows you the shape of your life: what you
photograph when you're happy, the months the pictures stop, the people who
appear and fade. It all runs locally.

## What you'll learn

Vision models and timeline analysis.

## What you'll need

> Finalized during the build. The list below is the starting assumption, not the
> final one.

- Python 3.10+
- An export of your camera roll, or a folder of photos with their dates intact
- A vision model, local or hosted
- An OpenAI API key if you use the hosted path
