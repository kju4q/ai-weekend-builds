# 05. Freedom Calculator

**Difficulty**: Advanced | **Time**: Full day

## What it is

Takes your income with its real variability, plus what you actually spend, and
simulates the next year ten thousand times. You change an input and watch how
the outcome moves. This is exploration from your own assumptions, not financial
advice.

## What you'll learn

Probabilistic thinking, volatility, percentiles, distributions.

## What you'll need

> Finalized during the build. The list below is the starting assumption, not the
> final one.

- Python 3.10+
- Your own numbers: income history and real spending
- No model key needed for the simulation itself
